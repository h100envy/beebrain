# limitations

beebrain is an architecture borrowed from biology. it is not a bee, and it does not give your desk an insect's intuition.

## what it does not do

- it is scaled down. a real bee has about 960,000 neurons. the model runs 2,000 kenyon cells and a few hundred drawn cells for display.
- it is not a connectome. there is no public whole brain honeybee wiring file to load. the lobes, the sparse code and the reward channel follow published biology, the numbers inside are mine.
- its scores are not probabilities. a mushroom value of 0.81 means the sparse code leans toward past prints. it is uncalibrated.
- it can only learn edges that exist. in both simulations the edge was planted. on a live chain the edge may be weaker, slower or gone.
- it does not trade for you. no keys, no signatures, no orders. you click.

## what the numbers mean

every number on screen is computed by the running simulation. the pools are synthetic and the account is paper.
the ten seed table shows that the brain can find an edge that exists in the generator. it says nothing about a real market.

the spread across seeds is the honest part. some seeds grow cautious early and go nowhere, some compound hard.
one seed is not a result.

## stage 2 and stage 3

`--post 2` uses a rule based reader in place of grok. it is not the grok api. the link between noise and rugs is a model assumption.

`--post 3` plants a telegram velocity edge. the bee finding it proves the mechanism, not the market.

## live account mode

`--trades` plays back your own closed trades next to the animation. the brain does not learn from them and does not score them.
the animation still runs on synthetic pools. the header says so.

## the field

- the live features are proxies. pool age stands in for creator history, buys and sell pressure for holders and snipers. none of them is the planted edge of the sim.
- the bee arrives with an empty memory and a 50% explore rate. its first hours on a chain are mostly noise.
- paper fills use the quoted price plus a depth based impact and a 1% fee each way. real fills on thin pools can be much worse, and a paper sell always goes through. a real honeypot does not let you out.
- prices arrive every 15 seconds at best. a rug inside that window shows up late.
- bonding curve launches without a pool are skipped by a reflex. the bee never trades them.
- discovery comes from dexscreener's latest profiles and boosts and geckoterminal's newest pools. that is a sample, biased toward tokens someone paid to promote.
- the forward test is honest but young. under a few hundred pools per verdict, its hit rates are mostly luck.

## the 3d video

the video uses the engine for the pools, verdicts and scores it shows, from one seed and one window of pools.
the point cloud is a schematic of bee brain anatomy, not a scan.
